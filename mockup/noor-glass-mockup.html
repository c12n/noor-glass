Noor Glass

Standalone Quran app split out of Prayer YEG. Same deep green and gold Quran palette throughout, by design. iOS gets true Liquid Glass: translucent glass cards, a floating glass tab bar and a glass mini player over a soft light aurora. Android gets Material 3 Expressive: expressive shapes, a wavy progress bar, dynamic green colour and big pill controls. Pick a direction, or mix and match rows.
